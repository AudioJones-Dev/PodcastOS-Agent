# Production SOP

## Purpose

Record the approved episode and preserve accurate production notes for post-production.

## Trigger

A production cycle begins when:

- Pre-production is complete
- The recording date arrives
- The operator confirms shoot readiness

## Inputs

- Approved production sheet
- Recording-readiness checklist
- Host brief
- Equipment checklist
- Recording link or location
- File naming rules

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Approval and Readiness

Verify the episode is approved and all critical logistics are ready.

### Step 2 - Record Episode

Record the long-form episode according to the production sheet and operator direction.

### Step 3 - Capture Notes

Log strong moments, retakes, technical issues, timestamps, and follow-up tasks.

### Step 4 - Transfer Raw Files

Move or link raw files into the episode folder according to the client folder schema.

### Step 5 - Hand Off to Post-Production

Create an ingest note with file location, version, and production issues.

## Outputs

- production-notes.md
- raw-file-ingest-note.md
- technical-issues.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when raw files are accounted for and post-production has a clear handoff.
