# Repurposing SOP

## Purpose

Create derivative content from an approved episode and transcript.

## Trigger

Repurposing begins when:

- The episode transcript is available
- Post-production has a review-ready or approved export
- The operator requests derivative content

## Inputs

- Transcript
- Transcript insights
- Production sheet
- Client Bible
- Offer map
- Distribution channels
- Brand guidelines

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Review Source Episode

Review the transcript, production sheet, and strategic goal.

### Step 2 - Extract Content Angles

Identify quotes, hooks, questions, clip opportunities, blog angles, and social themes.

### Step 3 - Draft Derivative Assets

Create show notes, blog draft, captions, clip titles, newsletter angle, and quote prompts as needed.

### Step 4 - Align CTA

Confirm CTA and link targets match the offer map.

### Step 5 - Package for Approval

Prepare assets for operator or client review.

## Outputs

- show-notes.md
- blog.md
- social-posts.md
- clip-package.md
- newsletter-draft.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when derivative assets are drafted, organized, and ready for publishing approval.
