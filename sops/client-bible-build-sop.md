# Client Bible Build SOP

## Purpose

Convert approved discovery material into a Client Production Bible that agents can use as the source of truth.

## Trigger

A Client Bible build begins when:

- Discovery is approved
- A client is newly onboarded
- Existing Client Bible files are missing or outdated
- The operator requests a strategic refresh

## Inputs

- Approved discovery summary
- Client profile details
- Brand guidelines
- Offer map
- ICP notes
- Messaging notes
- SEO/AEO research
- Existing content inventory

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Folder Structure

Validate that the client folder matches the client folder schema.

### Step 2 - Populate Client Profile

Document business overview, audience, service area, goals, constraints, and owner/operator notes.

### Step 3 - Build Offer Map

Document offers, value propositions, conversion paths, objections, and CTAs.

### Step 4 - Build ICP Profile

Document ideal client traits, pains, triggers, objections, and decision criteria.

### Step 5 - Add Messaging and Brand Rules

Capture voice, tone, words to use, words to avoid, claims requiring approval, and visual rules.

### Step 6 - Mark Gaps

Flag missing assets, missing research, open strategic questions, and approval needs.

## Outputs

- client-profile.md
- brand-guidelines.md
- offer-map.md
- ideal-client-profile.md
- messaging-notes.md
- discovery-summary.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when the Client Bible exists, all required files are initialized, and missing inputs are clearly flagged.
