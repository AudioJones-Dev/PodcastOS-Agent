# Analytics SOP

## Purpose

Review performance data and convert it into clear reporting and next-cycle recommendations.

## Trigger

Analytics begins when:

- The reporting window closes
- The operator requests a performance review
- A monthly report is due
- Topic refresh needs supporting data

## Inputs

- Distribution log
- Platform analytics
- Website analytics
- Search data
- Social engagement data
- Campaign goals
- Client Bible

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Reporting Window

Define the period covered by the report.

### Step 2 - Gather Channel Metrics

Collect views, listens, watch time, engagement, clicks, conversions, rankings, or other available metrics.

### Step 3 - Compare Against Goals

Tie results back to awareness, authority, search, lead generation, education, or trust-building objectives.

### Step 4 - Summarize Findings

Document strongest assets, weak assets, audience signals, and notable changes.

### Step 5 - Recommend Next Moves

Create next-cycle content and distribution recommendations.

### Step 6 - Request Report Approval

Send the report for human approval before client delivery.

## Outputs

- analytics.md
- performance-summary.md
- monthly-report.md
- next-cycle-recommendations.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when the report is drafted, recommendations are documented, and approval is requested.
