# Distribution Admin SOP

## Purpose

Maintain the official publishing and distribution record for each episode.

## Trigger

Distribution administration begins when:

- An asset is published
- An asset is scheduled
- A publishing handoff is received
- The operator requests URL reconciliation

## Inputs

- Publishing handoff
- Channel list
- Published URLs
- Scheduled URLs
- Publish dates
- Asset names

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Review Publishing Handoff

Confirm the list of assets expected for distribution.

### Step 2 - Log URLs

Record URL, platform, status, publish date, and asset type.

### Step 3 - Reconcile Missing Items

Flag any channel or asset without a URL or scheduled status.

### Step 4 - Mark Analytics Readiness

Notify Analytics Agent when tracking can begin.

### Step 5 - Update Client Records

Ensure distribution records are linked from the episode folder or Client Bible.

## Outputs

- distribution-log.md
- missing-distribution-note.md
- analytics-readiness-note.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when all approved assets are logged or clearly marked pending, blocked, or not applicable.
