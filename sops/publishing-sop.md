# Publishing SOP

## Purpose

Publish or schedule approved podcast and derivative content across selected channels.

## Trigger

Publishing begins when:

- Publishing approval is granted
- Approved long-form and derivative assets are ready
- Channel access is available
- Publishing date or schedule is confirmed

## Inputs

- Approved episode export
- Approved thumbnail
- Approved title and description
- Approved blog or show notes
- Approved captions and clips
- CTA links
- Channel requirements

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Approval

Verify publishing approval and approved asset versions.

### Step 2 - Validate Metadata

Check title, description, tags, thumbnail, CTA, links, and scheduled date.

### Step 3 - Prepare Channel Uploads

Format assets according to YouTube, podcast host, website, social, or newsletter requirements.

### Step 4 - Publish or Schedule

Upload or schedule assets according to operator instructions.

### Step 5 - Hand Off URLs

Send live or scheduled URLs to Distribution Admin Agent.

## Outputs

- publishing-checklist.md
- platform-metadata.md
- schedule-status.md
- publishing-handoff.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when approved assets are published or scheduled and the distribution handoff is ready.
