# Topic Refresh SOP

## Purpose

Update the topic strategy based on analytics, client priorities, and market signals.

## Trigger

A topic refresh begins when:

- Analytics are reviewed
- A new month or production cycle begins
- A trend or market shift appears
- A client's offer or ICP changes
- Topic fatigue is detected

## Inputs

- Analytics report
- Topic clusters
- Episode recommendations
- Client Bible
- Offer map
- Search and AEO notes
- Market or trend notes

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Review Current Topic Strategy

Compare current topic clusters against the Client Bible and recent performance data.

### Step 2 - Identify Winners and Weaknesses

Mark high-performing topics, low-performing topics, and underdeveloped authority areas.

### Step 3 - Update Topic Scores

Adjust scoring based on business relevance, demand, ICP fit, offer alignment, and clip potential.

### Step 4 - Add New Opportunities

Add timely topics, trend opportunities, search questions, or follow-up angles.

### Step 5 - Recommend Next Cycle

Create prioritized recommendations for the next production cycle.

### Step 6 - Request Strategic Approval

Request approval before changing content direction.

## Outputs

- refreshed-topic-clusters.md
- updated-topic-score-table.md
- next-cycle-topic-recommendations.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when refreshed topic recommendations are documented and submitted for approval.
