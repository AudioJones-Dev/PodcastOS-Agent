# Pre-Production Meeting SOP

## Purpose

Align the host, guest, operator, and production team before recording.

## Trigger

A pre-production meeting begins when:

- The production sheet is approved
- A recording date is scheduled
- A guest requires briefing
- The operator requests a readiness check

## Inputs

- Approved production sheet
- Host brief
- Episode outline
- Guest details
- Recording date and location
- Equipment checklist
- Asset requirements

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Episode Goal

Review the episode's audience, core promise, CTA, and desired outcome.

### Step 2 - Review Recording Flow

Confirm intro, major talking points, guest flow, close, and backup prompts.

### Step 3 - Confirm Logistics

Verify location, remote link, time zone, equipment, file naming, and backup plan.

### Step 4 - Confirm Brand and Claim Rules

Review any claims, topics, or language that need special care.

### Step 5 - Log Readiness

Document blockers, decisions, and final readiness state.

## Outputs

- pre-production-notes.md
- recording-readiness-checklist.md
- blocker-log.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when the episode is marked ready to record or blocked with a documented next action.
