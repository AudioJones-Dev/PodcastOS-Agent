# Topic Strategy SOP

## Purpose

Create podcast topic clusters that support brand authority, search visibility, social reach, and client acquisition.

## Trigger

A topic strategy cycle begins when:

- A new client completes discovery
- A new month begins
- Analytics indicate topic fatigue
- A trend or news opportunity appears
- The client's offer or market changes

## Inputs

- Client Bible
- Offer map
- ICP
- Audience pain points
- Search queries
- AEO questions
- Competitor topics
- Analytics from previous episodes

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Review Client Context

Review:

- Client profile
- Brand guidelines
- Offers
- ICP
- Messaging notes
- Previous topics

### Step 2 - Research Queries

Identify:

- Search keywords
- Question-based queries
- Buying-intent queries
- Educational queries
- Comparison queries
- Local or industry-specific queries

### Step 3 - Map Pain Points

Connect each query or topic to:

- ICP pain point
- Desired outcome
- Client offer
- Authority position

### Step 4 - Build Clusters

Group related topics into clusters.

Each cluster should include:

- Cluster name
- Main theme
- Target audience
- Search intent
- Episode ideas
- CTA alignment

### Step 5 - Score Topics

Score each topic by:

- Business relevance
- Search/AEO demand
- ICP fit
- Offer alignment
- Social clip potential

### Step 6 - Recommend Episodes

Create a ranked list of recommended episodes.

## Outputs

- topic-clusters.md
- topic-score-table.md
- episode-recommendations.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when at least one approved topic cluster is ready for episode planning.
