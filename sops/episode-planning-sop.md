# Episode Planning SOP

## Purpose

Turn an approved topic into a production-ready episode plan.

## Trigger

An episode planning cycle begins when:

- A topic cluster is approved
- A new production cycle is opened
- Analytics recommend a follow-up topic
- A timely trend requires a planned response

## Inputs

- Approved topic cluster
- Episode recommendation
- Client Bible
- ICP
- Offer map
- Previous analytics
- Service-tier cadence

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Confirm Topic Approval

Verify that the selected topic has human approval.

### Step 2 - Define Episode Strategy

Document the strategic goal, audience, core problem, core promise, and CTA.

### Step 3 - Draft Episode Structure

Create talking points, title options, hook, outline, and segment flow.

### Step 4 - Identify Repurposing Angles

List clip opportunities, blog angle, social caption angle, and thumbnail direction.

### Step 5 - Create Host Brief

Summarize the recording purpose, audience, talking points, and cautions for the host.

### Step 6 - Request Approval

Submit the production sheet for approval before pre-production.

## Outputs

- production-sheet.md
- outline.md
- host-brief.md
- title-options.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when the production sheet, outline, and host brief are ready and submitted for approval.
