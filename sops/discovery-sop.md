# Discovery SOP

## Purpose

Collect the strategic context needed to build a Client Production Bible and plan useful podcast content.

## Trigger

A discovery cycle begins when:

- A new client has been accepted
- A prospect needs qualification
- A client's business, offer, or market has changed
- The operator requests an updated strategy review

## Inputs

- Intake summary
- Discovery questionnaire
- Discovery call notes
- Website and social links
- Existing content examples
- Competitor references
- Brand assets

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Review Intake

Review intake details and identify missing context before the discovery session.

### Step 2 - Capture Business Context

Document the client's business model, offers, goals, constraints, and strategic priorities.

### Step 3 - Capture Audience Context

Document ideal client profile, buyer pain points, common questions, objections, and desired outcomes.

### Step 4 - Capture Content Context

Review existing podcast, video, blog, social, and newsletter assets.

### Step 5 - Identify Open Questions

List unclear claims, missing assets, incomplete offers, and decisions that require client confirmation.

### Step 6 - Request Approval

Submit the discovery summary for human approval before Client Bible build.

## Outputs

- discovery-summary.md
- open-questions.md
- client-bible-inputs.md

## Completion Criteria

The SOP is complete when discovery has been summarized, open questions are documented, and the operator approves the discovery record for Client Bible build.
